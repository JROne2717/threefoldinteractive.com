About the studio

Threefold Interactive LLC is an independently owned and operated game development studio. We're not backed by a publisher and we're not chasing a trend — we're a small team making the kind of games we want to play.

Everything we ship is built in-house. That means slower releases, but it also means we answer to players rather than to a roadmap someone else wrote.
