Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This policy explains what information Threefold Interactive LLC ("Threefold Interactive", "we", "us") collects, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. It covers this website and any games or applications we publish.

The short version

We don't sell your personal information and we don't run ads. Broken Divide collects what it needs to save your progress, run leaderboards and raids, and fix crashes — all of it listed below. Leaderboard scores are public; nothing else you send us is.

Information we collect

Information you give us directly. If you email or call us, we receive whatever you choose to send — your email address, phone number, and the contents of your message. We keep that correspondence so we can follow up on it.

Information collected automatically on this website. This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on it. GitHub, as our hosting provider, may log standard technical request data such as IP addresses; see GitHub's Privacy Statement for how they handle it.

Information collected by Broken Divide. When you play the game, it collects:

  • Your Steam account identifier. Steam gives the game a numeric ID for your account. We use it to tie your saves, achievements, and leaderboard entries to you.
  • Save and progress data, so your progress follows you between machines.
  • Achievement progress, recorded through Steam.
  • Rift results — how far you got and the score it earned, uploaded so it can be placed on a leaderboard.
  • Raid damage totals — the damage you deal in an asynchronous raid, uploaded and counted against that boss's health so your contribution adds to the shared result.
  • Crash reports, which include what the game was doing when it failed, plus technical details about your machine such as operating system version and hardware specification.
  • Gameplay analytics — things like session length, how far players get, and which features get used — so we can find where the game is confusing, too hard, or broken.

Broken Divide never asks for your name, address, or payment details. Your purchase is handled entirely by Steam and we never see your payment information.

What other players can see

Leaderboards are public. That is the point of them — but it means the score you post and the Steam persona name attached to your account can be seen by other players. Your contribution to an asynchronous raid works the same way.

Nothing else you send us is public. Your saves, crash reports, and analytics are never shown to other players. If you would rather not appear on a leaderboard at all, you can change the persona name Steam displays at any time in your Steam profile settings.

Third-party services

Valve Corporation (Steam). Broken Divide is distributed through Steam and uses Steam's platform features for sign-in, achievements, and cloud saves. What Valve does with your information is covered by Valve's Privacy Policy.

Crash reporting and analytics. We use third-party services to collect crash reports and gameplay analytics. They process that information on our behalf, under contract, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes or to sell it.

How we use information

  • To save your progress and sync it between your machines
  • To run leaderboards, rift rankings, and asynchronous raids
  • To record achievements
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • To understand how the game is played so we can balance and improve it
  • To respond to questions, support requests, and business inquiries
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations

We do not sell or rent personal information, we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

How long we keep it

Saves and progress data are kept while your copy of the game is active, so you don't lose your game. Leaderboard and raid entries are kept for as long as that leaderboard or raid event is running. Crash reports and analytics are kept only as long as they're useful for fixing and balancing the game, and are then deleted or aggregated so they no longer identify anyone.

We keep correspondence for as long as it's useful for support and business records, and then delete it. Where the law requires us to retain records — tax and payment records, for example — we keep them for the period required and no longer.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your personal information, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, or ask us to transfer it. Residents of the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom have these rights under the GDPR; California residents have comparable rights under the CCPA and CPRA, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

To exercise any of these, email admin@threefoldinteractive.com. We'll respond within 30 days. See our data deletion instructions for the fastest route to a deletion request.

One thing worth knowing about deletion: removing your data also removes your leaderboard and raid entries, and we can't put them back. If you ask us to delete and later play again, you start from nothing.

Children

Our website and games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security — but we limit what we collect, which limits what can be exposed.

International transfers

We operate in the United States. If you contact us from outside the US, the information you send will be processed in the US, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the "last updated" date above. Material changes — particularly any new collection of personal information — will be posted here before they take effect.
